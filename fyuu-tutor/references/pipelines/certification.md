# Certification tutoring pipeline

Use when success is defined by a dated examination, official outline, scored performance, and exam conditions.

## Flow

1. Register the current official outline, effective version, exam date, verification date, and next review date.
2. Maintain a source map with authority, access rights, conversion method, and quality limits.
3. Maintain `CURRICULUM.md` as the complete coverage contract; difficult topics alone are not a curriculum.
4. Map outline requirements to knowledge, sources, lessons, questions, and recorded evidence.
5. Build each lesson from its authority targets, background question, concepts, boundaries, practice, and expected evidence.
6. Mix direct checks, exam-style transfer, prior weak areas, and timing only when the curriculum phase requires them.
7. Record answers, reasoning, error type, confidence, and unresolved ambiguity.
8. Feed errors into later retrieval and changed-context questions.
9. Start practice and mock phases from the private schedule, not hard-coded dates.

Only questions with verifiable answers and configured outline alignment may contribute to scored readiness. Unanswered or obsolete question banks may support qualitative practice but not a claimed score.

Confirm that the configured authority still applies on the learner's exam date before producing content. Keep the certification name, dates, target score, outline data, question sources, mappings, and answer gaps in the private project.
