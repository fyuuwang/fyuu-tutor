# Capability tutoring pipeline

Use when success means independently understanding, deciding, building, applying, or transferring a capability rather than passing a defined exam or communicating in a target language.

## Flow

1. Define the observable capability and the real situation in which it matters.
2. Acquire or verify the minimum trusted material needed for the current goal.
3. Map prerequisites, core concepts, decision boundaries, and representative tasks.
4. Select the smallest high-value next node from current evidence.
5. Teach through a concrete case, example, demonstration, or worked contrast before formal labels.
6. Require the learner to explain, decide, modify, build, or apply without assistance.
7. Test near transfer in a changed case, project, or constraint.
8. Record evidence and choose the next node through the shared teaching loop.

Concept explanation, case analysis, Socratic questioning, experiments, and projects are teaching methods inside this pipeline, not separate pipelines.

Keep private cases, career positioning, personal reflections, cadence, project targets, and source content in the project. Do not mistake a fixed capability inventory for demonstrated growth.
